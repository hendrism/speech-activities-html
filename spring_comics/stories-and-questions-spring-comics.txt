#SLP #TherapyTools

Below are all 10 stories in plain text format.
Each includes:

• short narrative
• 5 literal WH questions
• 5 inferential questions
• hint
• answer choices

The vocabulary stays clear and direct so students can focus on comprehension and reasoning.

⸻

1. The Watering Can

One afternoon, Sam and Mia were helping in the garden behind their school. The sun was warm, and colorful flowers were growing along the path.

Sam held a watering can and carefully poured water on the flowers. Mia stood nearby holding a small shovel.

While Sam was watering, he tipped the watering can too far. Water splashed forward and soaked Mia’s shirt.

Mia crossed her arms and looked upset. Sam quickly stopped watering and said, “I’m sorry.”

After a moment, Mia nodded. They went back to watering the flowers together.

⸻

Literal Questions
	1.	Who was helping in the garden?
Hint: Look at the first sentence.
Choices: Sam and Mia / Sam and a teacher / Mia and a dog
	2.	Where were they working?
Hint: It says where the flowers were.
Choices: in the classroom / in the garden behind the school / at the playground
	3.	What was Sam holding?
Hint: It helped water the flowers.
Choices: a watering can / a bucket / a hose
	4.	What happened to Mia?
Hint: Water moved forward.
Choices: she fell down / she got splashed with water / she dropped the flowers
	5.	What did Sam say?
Hint: He felt bad.
Choices: “I’m sorry.” / “Look at the flowers.” / “Let’s go inside.”

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did Mia cross her arms?
Hint: Think about how she felt.
Choices: she was annoyed about getting wet / she was tired / she was cold
	2.	Why did Sam look embarrassed?
Hint: Something he did caused the problem.
Choices: he splashed Mia / he broke the watering can / he lost the flowers
	3.	Why did Mia nod after Sam said sorry?
Hint: What does nodding often mean?
Choices: she accepted the apology / she wanted to go home / she was still angry
	4.	What kind of friend was Sam being at the end?
Hint: Think about what he said.
Choices: responsible / careless / mean
	5.	Why did they go back to watering the flowers?
Hint: The plants still needed something.
Choices: the flowers needed water / they wanted to leave / they were bored

2. The Dropped Ice Cream

Lena was walking through the park on a warm afternoon. She had just bought a chocolate ice cream cone.

As she walked along the path, she took a bite of the ice cream. Suddenly, the scoop slid off the cone and fell onto the ground.

Lena stopped walking and looked down at the melted ice cream.

She sighed and threw the empty cone in the trash. Then she walked back to the ice cream stand to get a new treat.

⸻

Literal Questions
	1.	Where was Lena walking?
Hint: It was outside.
Choices: the park / the classroom / the library
	2.	What did Lena buy?
Hint: It was a sweet treat.
Choices: a cookie / an ice cream cone / a sandwich
	3.	What happened to the ice cream?
Hint: It moved off the cone.
Choices: it melted in her hand / it fell on the ground / a bird took it
	4.	What did Lena look at?
Hint: It was on the ground.
Choices: the melted ice cream / a flower / her shoes
	5.	Where did Lena go at the end?
Hint: She wanted another treat.
Choices: home / the ice cream stand / the playground

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did the ice cream fall?
Hint: It may have been soft.
Choices: it was melting / she threw it / the wind blew it
	2.	How did Lena probably feel?
Hint: She lost her treat.
Choices: disappointed / excited / proud
	3.	Why did Lena sigh?
Hint: A sigh can show emotion.
Choices: she was upset / she was sleepy / she was hungry
	4.	Why did she go back to the stand?
Hint: Think about what she wanted.
Choices: to buy another ice cream / to go home / to talk to a friend
	5.	What might Lena do next?
Hint: She still wants something sweet.
Choices: buy another ice cream / leave the park / sit on the ground

3. Sharing the Ball

Jordan was playing with a red ball on the playground after school. He bounced the ball and tossed it into the air.

Nearby, Ava watched him play.

Ava walked closer and pointed to the ball. “Can I play too?” she asked.

Jordan smiled and handed her the ball. Soon they were both tossing it back and forth and laughing.

⸻

Literal Questions
	1.	What was Jordan playing with?
Hint: It was red.
Choices: a ball / a kite / a toy car
	2.	Where was Jordan?
Hint: Children often play there.
Choices: the playground / the classroom / the cafeteria
	3.	Who was watching Jordan?
Hint: Another child.
Choices: Ava / Mia / Sam
	4.	What did Ava ask?
Hint: She wanted to join.
Choices: “Can I play too?” / “Where is the ball?” / “What time is it?”
	5.	What did Jordan do?
Hint: He made a kind choice.
Choices: he shared the ball / he walked away / he hid the ball

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did Ava point at the ball?
Hint: She wanted something.
Choices: she wanted to play / she wanted to leave / she was confused
	2.	How did Jordan probably feel when they started playing together?
Hint: They were laughing.
Choices: happy / angry / bored
	3.	Why was sharing a good choice?
Hint: Think about friendship.
Choices: it allowed both kids to play / it stopped the game / it broke the ball
	4.	What kind of person was Jordan being?
Hint: Think about his behavior.
Choices: kind / selfish / careless
	5.	What might they do next?
Hint: They are playing a game.
Choices: keep playing / go inside / stop talking

4. The Kite in the Tree

Ethan was flying a colorful kite in the park. The wind was strong and the kite flew high in the sky.

Suddenly, the kite drifted toward a tree. The string pulled tight, and the kite got stuck in the branches.

Ethan looked up at the tree and frowned.

He carefully pulled on the string and tried to free the kite.

⸻

Literal Questions
	1.	What was Ethan flying?
Hint: It was in the sky.
Choices: a kite / a balloon / a drone
	2.	Where was Ethan?
Hint: It is a place with trees.
Choices: the park / the classroom / the store
	3.	What happened to the kite?
Hint: It touched a tree.
Choices: it got stuck in a tree / it fell in water / it broke
	4.	What did Ethan do when the kite got stuck?
Hint: He looked upward.
Choices: looked up at the tree / ran home / dropped the string
	5.	What did Ethan try to do?
Hint: He pulled something.
Choices: free the kite / cut the string / leave the park

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did the kite get stuck?
Hint: Think about wind.
Choices: the wind pushed it into the tree / Ethan threw it / the string broke
	2.	How did Ethan probably feel?
Hint: He frowned.
Choices: frustrated / excited / relaxed
	3.	Why did he pull the string carefully?
Hint: He wanted something.
Choices: to free the kite / to break it / to drop it
	4.	What might Ethan try next?
Hint: He still wants the kite.
Choices: climb the tree / walk away / buy a new kite
	5.	Why was the kite important to Ethan?
Hint: He was playing with it.
Choices: it was his toy / it belonged to a teacher / it was trash

6. Jumping in the Puddle

After school, Maya and Lucas were walking along a path in the park. It had rained earlier, and there were puddles on the ground.

Maya stopped when she saw a big puddle in the middle of the path.

With a laugh, she jumped right into it. Water splashed everywhere.

Some of the water splashed onto Lucas. He stepped back and looked surprised.

Maya looked down at the puddle and realized Lucas had gotten wet too. She apologized, and they continued walking down the path.

⸻

Literal Questions
	1.	Where were Maya and Lucas walking?
Hint: It was outside in nature.
Choices: the park / the classroom / the grocery store
	2.	What was on the ground?
Hint: It formed after rain.
Choices: puddles / leaves / rocks
	3.	What did Maya do when she saw the puddle?
Hint: She moved into it.
Choices: she jumped into it / she walked around it / she kicked it
	4.	What happened to Lucas?
Hint: Water reached him.
Choices: he got splashed / he fell down / he ran away
	5.	What did Maya say after she noticed Lucas was wet?
Hint: She felt sorry.
Choices: she apologized / she laughed again / she ignored him

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did Maya jump in the puddle?
Hint: Think about how she reacted.
Choices: she thought it would be fun / she was angry / she was tired
	2.	Why was Lucas surprised?
Hint: Something unexpected happened.
Choices: he got splashed with water / he saw a bird / he found a toy
	3.	Why did Maya apologize?
Hint: Think about Lucas.
Choices: she realized she got him wet / she wanted to go home / she broke something
	4.	How did Lucas probably feel when he got splashed?
Hint: His reaction showed emotion.
Choices: surprised / excited / bored
	5.	What might they do next?
Hint: They were walking together.
Choices: keep walking / sit in the puddle / go back to school

7. The Broken Toy

Oliver was sitting on the floor in his room playing with his toy car. He pushed the car across the floor and made engine sounds.

Suddenly, one of the wheels popped off the toy.

Oliver picked up the car and looked at the loose wheel in his hand.

He felt disappointed because he liked that toy.

After a moment, Oliver decided to take the car to his dad to see if they could fix it together.

⸻

Literal Questions
	1.	What was Oliver playing with?
Hint: It had wheels.
Choices: a toy car / a ball / a kite
	2.	Where was Oliver sitting?
Hint: It was inside his home.
Choices: his room / the park / the school hallway
	3.	What happened to the toy car?
Hint: One part came off.
Choices: a wheel popped off / the car melted / it disappeared
	4.	What did Oliver pick up?
Hint: It was part of the toy.
Choices: the loose wheel / a book / a shoe
	5.	Who did Oliver decide to go see?
Hint: Someone who could help.
Choices: his dad / his teacher / a friend

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did Oliver feel disappointed?
Hint: Something happened to his toy.
Choices: the toy broke / he lost a game / he dropped his book
	2.	Why did Oliver take the car to his dad?
Hint: He needed help.
Choices: to fix the toy / to throw it away / to buy a new one
	3.	What might Oliver and his dad do next?
Hint: Think about the broken wheel.
Choices: try to fix the car / hide the toy / paint the toy
	4.	Why was the toy important to Oliver?
Hint: He was playing with it.
Choices: it was one of his favorite toys / it was trash / it belonged to a stranger
	5.	What kind of problem did Oliver have?
Hint: Think about the toy.
Choices: a broken toy / a lost pet / a missing backpack

The wind started to blow harder.

Suddenly, a strong gust lifted the hat right off Daniel’s head.

The hat rolled along the path in front of him.

Daniel ran after it to catch it before it blew away.

⸻

Literal Questions

Hint: It was on his head.
Choices: a baseball cap / a scarf / a helmet
	2.	Where was Daniel walking?
Hint: It had trees and paths.
Choices: the park / the classroom / the mall
	3.	What caused the hat to move?
Hint: It was weather.
Choices: the wind / a dog / a bird
	4.	What happened to the hat?
Hint: It left his head.
Choices: it blew off / it broke / it disappeared
	5.	What did Daniel do next?
Hint: He moved quickly.
Choices: ran after the hat / sat down / called a friend

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did the wind blow the hat away?
Hint: The wind was strong.
Choices: a gust lifted it / Daniel threw it / someone grabbed it
	2.	Why did Daniel run after the hat?
Hint: It belonged to him.
Choices: he wanted to get it back / he wanted to lose it / he wanted to buy another
	3.	How did Daniel probably feel?
Hint: His hat was moving away.
Choices: worried / excited / sleepy
	4.	What might happen if he did not catch it?
Hint: Think about the wind.
Choices: the hat might blow away / the hat might grow / the hat might disappear
	5.	Why was the hat important?
Hint: The story says something about it.
Choices: it was his favorite hat / it was trash / it was broken

10. Picking Up the Books

Sofia was walking down the school hallway carrying several books in her arms.

As she turned the corner, the books slipped from her hands and fell onto the floor.

A student named Liam saw the books fall.

Liam walked over and helped Sofia pick up the books.

Sofia smiled and thanked him for helping.

⸻

Literal Questions
	1.	Where was Sofia walking?
Hint: It was inside the school.
Choices: the hallway / the playground / the cafeteria
	2.	What was Sofia carrying?
Hint: Students read them.
Choices: books / pencils / lunch trays
	3.	What happened to the books?
Hint: They moved downward.
Choices: they fell on the floor / they disappeared / they broke
	4.	Who saw the books fall?
Hint: Another student.
Choices: Liam / Sam / Emma
	5.	What did Liam do?
Hint: He helped.
Choices: picked up the books / walked away / kicked the books

⸻

Inferential Questions
	1.	Why did the books fall?
Hint: Sofia was holding many items.
Choices: they slipped from her arms / someone pushed them / they were thrown
	2.	Why did Liam help Sofia?
Hint: Think about kindness.
Choices: he wanted to help / he wanted the books / he was bored
	3.	How did Sofia probably feel when Liam helped?
Hint: She smiled.
Choices: grateful / angry / confused
	4.	What kind of action did Liam show?
Hint: Think about helping others.
Choices: kindness / selfishness / carelessness
	5.	What might Sofia do after Liam helps?
Hint: She spoke to him.
Choices: say thank you / drop the books again / leave the school
