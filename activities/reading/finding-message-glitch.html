Finding the Message Glitch

Types of Message Glitches

🧍 Speaker Glitch

Delivery issues: Too fast, too quiet, not facing you, confusing words.

👂 Listener Glitch

Attention issues: Distracted, missed a word, assumed interpretation.

🌪 Environment Glitch

Room issues: Noisy, too far away, bad connection, interruptions.

🧩 Message Glitch

Content issues: Vague words ("that", "it"), missing details, confusing directions.
