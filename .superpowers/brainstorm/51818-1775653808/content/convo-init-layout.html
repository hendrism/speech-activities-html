Merged Conversation Initiation — How should modes be organized?

The 4 current activities have different scaffolding levels. How should the clinician switch between them?

A

Tab bar at the top

Three tabs always visible: Quick (write a starter + predict) / Guided (step-by-step builder) / Multi-Exchange (add multiple back-and-forth turns). Clinician clicks a tab to switch modes. Scenario selector sits below the tabs.

B

Mode picker on load

Activity opens with a full-screen mode picker — clinician chooses the scaffolding level before starting. Once chosen, the activity runs in that mode. A small "Change mode" button in the header lets them switch mid-session if needed.

Left sidebar contains both the mode selector (radio buttons or small tabs) and the scenario list. Main panel shows the activity. Everything visible at once — clinician can see where they are and switch without leaving the activity.
